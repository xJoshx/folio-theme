# Brand direction

`Folio` is the release name. It joins the editorial meaning of a sheet or
volume with the botanical meaning of a leaf, giving the family a natural,
tactile identity without suggesting an affiliation with Material Design.

## Name shortlist

- **Folio** — tactile, editorial, and broad enough to hold the whole
  four-theme system. Logo route: layered leaves or paper planes around a
  small editor-square/cursor motif.
- **Pigment** — direct, colour-led, and closely connected to the Wada origin.
  Logo route: three overlapping swatches or print-registration shapes.
- **Folio** — quiet, editorial, and archival. Logo route: a folded page, leaf,
  or herbarium specimen reduced to a compact glyph.
- **Bloom** — expressive and memorable, with the strongest connection to the
  botanical palette. Logo route: an abstract three-part bloom with a square or
  cursor at its centre; avoid a literal flower mark.

The final name will still be confirmed together with its logo, not as an isolated word.
Each candidate should therefore be tested as both a standalone icon and a
wordmark under the same constraints.

## Product concept

The identity has to accommodate three dark personalities and a shared light surface:

- **Luminous Ink / Graphite** — editorial.
- **Sunlit Shell / Mineral Slate** — bright and mineral.
- **Spring Herbarium / Tyrian Night** — creative and botanical.
- **Linen** — quiet, neutral, and light.

The family should feel crafted and colour-conscious without becoming nostalgic
or ornamental. Sanzo Wada is a palette source, not part of the public name or a
claim of affiliation.

## Logo evaluation criteria

- Recognisable at 16–32 px as a Zed extension icon.
- Still works in one colour and without the wordmark.
- Legible on Graphite, Mineral Slate, Tyrian Night, and the light counterparts.
- Can represent all three themes rather than privileging only one.
- Avoids literal flowers, faux-Japanese motifs, and generic code brackets.
- Distinct enough for a marketplace tile, repository avatar, and social image.
